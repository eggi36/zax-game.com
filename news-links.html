Site News

11/18/26Seven early screenshots of January 2001 – the series of the developer's site, also shown by GameSpot – are now in the Archive, also on the screenshot pages of the 2001 versions. The first Zax page of 1998 shows its small pictures again, scaled down from the Score cover CD pictures, and the screenshot gallery of 2003 has four more pictures, found on jowood.com.

11/17/26New in Downloads: links to the full version of the game in seven languages with a tip for copy protection problems, a CD key generator and a fix pack for the original Russian edition.

11/17/26New finds from the Internet Archive: the first Zax page of 1998 and the reflexive.net page of 2003 in the Archive, many more pages and pictures of zax-game.com and jowood.com, and a new The Game page that brings together everything the developer's and the official site said about the game: story, characters, weapons, interface and screenshots.

11/16/26New in Downloads: the demo of 1 June 2001, the earliest known build of the game, in 8-bit colour and with still images instead of the intro videos.

11/16/26New in the Archive: ten early pictures of the game from the cover CD of the Czech magazine Score, issue 59 (November 1998), with the text that came with them.

11/14/26This fan site goes online: navigation and fonts rebuilt from the original Flash files of zax-game.com, the original websites restored in the Archive.

Development News 2000–2001

04/03/01Zax™ has a brand new name! "Zax the Alien Hunter" is now the official title of the Zax title that has been under construction for the past year. The team is currently working on multi-player maps, AI's and optimizations. The game is looking beautiful if I do say so myself.

02/15/01Hmmm, I wonder what's new? You guessed it! The Zax page has been redesigned yet again. However this time it is for good reason. There is now a Zax message board that will be moderated by members of Reflexive. There will be a major update to the Logs section soon.

01/03/01Well, the Zax Team is back at work after a nice relaxing vacation! There have been many changes, you just can't see most of them. There is a new screen shot, and the Logs section has been redone. There is also a new Weapon revealed, so have fun checking out all the new stuff.

12/07/00The website has just about been completed. All of the sections will be updated on a continuous basis. So checkout what is new and come back often for updates!

11/12/00The Zax website is going through some major changes. It should now be updated every week with the latest and greatest information on the Epic adventure Zax. Stay tuned for more information.

News from the official site by Reflexive Entertainment, 2000–2001.
