Development news 2000–2001

04/03/01Zax™ has a brand new name! "Zax the Alien Hunter" is now the official title of the Zax title that has been under construction for the past year. The team is currently working on multi-player maps, AI's and optimizations. The game is looking beautiful if I do say so myself.

02/15/01Hmmm, I wonder what's new? You guessed it! The Zax page has been redesigned yet again. However this time it is for good reason. There is now a Zax message board that will be moderated by members of Reflexive. There will be a major update to the Logs section soon.

01/03/01Well, the Zax Team is back at work after a nice relaxing vacation! There have been many changes, you just can't see most of them. There is a new screen shot, and the Logs section has been redone. There is also a new Weapon revealed, so have fun checking out all the new stuff.

12/07/00The website has just about been completed. All of the sections will be updated on a continuous basis. So checkout what is new and come back often for updates!

11/12/00The Zax website is going through some major changes. It should now be updated every week with the latest and greatest information on the Epic adventure Zax. Stay tuned for more information.

News from the official site by Reflexive Entertainment, 2000–2001.
