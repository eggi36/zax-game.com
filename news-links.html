Development News 1998–2001

2001-04-03Zax™ has a brand new name! "Zax the Alien Hunter" is now the official title of the Zax title that has been under construction for the past year. The team is currently working on multi-player maps, AI's and optimizations. The game is looking beautiful if I do say so myself.

2001-02-15Hmmm, I wonder what's new? You guessed it! The Zax page has been redesigned yet again. However this time it is for good reason. There is now a Zax message board that will be moderated by members of Reflexive. There will be a major update to the Logs section soon.

2001-01-03Well, the Zax Team is back at work after a nice relaxing vacation! There have been many changes, you just can't see most of them. There is a new screen shot, and the Logs section has been redone. There is also a new Weapon revealed, so have fun checking out all the new stuff.

2000-12-07The website has just about been completed. All of the sections will be updated on a continuous basis. So checkout what is new and come back often for updates!

2000-11-12The Zax website is going through some major changes. It should now be updated every week with the latest and greatest information on the Epic adventure Zax. Stay tuned for more information.

1998-12-22We're hiring! We are on the verge of expansion, and we are ready to add personnel for the exciting growth we have planned. We are currently seeking a full-time Artist and will be hiring a full-time Level Designer in the next few months to work on our next project, tentatively titled Zax. We have a great work environment and if you think you have what it takes, please inquire within...

1998-09-28Many new pictures have been added to the Zax page, as well as multiple hi-res screenshots. Just click on the low-res screenshots to get the larger version. Swarm is also out in selected stores, and will be getting substantial press in PC Gamer's December issue. We may have some more exciting news about our newest title Zax, but certain discussions are still hush hush at the moment. We will write more about it here as details become available, but it DEFINATELY looks like everyone will be hearing about Zax and Reflexive Entertainment in the near future...

1998-08-31A rendering of Om has been added to the Zax Web Page, as well as new story elements. Swarm will also be in stores “very soon”...hopefully late September. We will be adding pages and information on where to obtain the game in the near future.

1998-08 (late)Zax has been in development for over 4 months, and it is looking fantastic. For a sneak peak at our graphically intense gaming masterpiece, click HERE.

1998-05-28E3 in Atlanta, Georgia (May 28 through 30). The team had fun and garnered even more contacts within the industry. Had some extremely favorable contacts with the preliminary design of Zax.

1998-04-02The birth of a new game... Reflexive begins development of a brand new title called Zax. Our site will soon have detailed information on this exciting new game. Discover the Universe of Zax.

News from the developer's site by Reflexive Entertainment: 2000–2001 from the Zax page, 1998 from the Reflexive news page (the entries about Zax).
