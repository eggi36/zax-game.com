WALLPAPERS

To download on a PC:
Click the image that you would like to set as your wallpaper. The image will appear in a new window. Click on the right hand button on your mouse and scroll down to set it as your wallpaper.

To download on a Mac:
Click on the image that you would like to set as your wallpaper. The image will appear in a new window. Click on this new image and keep the mouse button held down so that you can drag the image onto your desktop. Open Desktop Pictures within Control Panels (System 7.x) or Appearance within Control Panels (System 8.x) and insert the picture.
