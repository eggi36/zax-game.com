FEATURES

Zax - The Alien Hunter is a non-stop action/adventure game that crash-lands you onto an alien world where everything has been turned upside down. A proud people have been enslaved, their world of ancient magic wrested from them by a mysterious god of metal. Your fiery entrance onto their planet has given them hope for a release from their torment, but you are in sad shape yourself. With no military training and a heavily-damaged ship, your obstacles are clear.

Unprepared as you are, you represent the final hope for the Korbo. Are you up to the challenge? Can you defeat the minions of Om? No one said being a hero was easy.

Over the course of more than 100 maps and 7 unique environment types, your main goal is simple: stay alive! If you can do that and somehow find a way to save the dying hopes of the Korbo, you will have met the challenge that is before you.

THE HEADS UP DISPLAY (HUD)

At all times during gameplay, you will see the HUD at the bottom of the screen. There, useful information is constantly displayed. Information available to you in the HUD (moving from left to right) is as follows:
 
 


 
  Health
If you allow Zax's health to go to 0% he dies and the game is over. You will want to keep his health as high as possible at all times.

Shield Charge
Zax's current shield charge is displayed as a graphical representation of the fraction of the current charge over the total possible charge.
Generating a higher level shield will increase total possible charge.

Ore & Crystals
The number of ore and crystals are displayed as a fraction of how many Zax currently has -compared to how many he has collected over the course of his adventure.

Sphere of Zin
This sphere detects evil in the general vicinity of its carrier.

Game Time Elapsed
Zelon will keep a running clock of how long Zax's adventure has taken him. Useful for the mission log.

Secondary weapon
Proximity Mines are displayed here (if Zax has them). The number below the icon represents the number of mines that Zax has in his possession. By default, weapons in this window are activated by clicking the right mouse button.

Battery charge
Zax's current battery charge is displayed as a graphical representation of the fraction of current over total possible charge. Generating a higher-level battery will increase total possible charge.

Primary Weapon
The primary weapon that Zax is currently using. By default, weapons in this window are fired by clicking the left mouse button

  
 

 
  THE MATERIALS GENERATOR

Common back on Zax's home planet, Materials Generators convert raw materials into other items. As Zelon develops new items for Zax to use on his quest, she will let him know of their availability.
Each item requires a specific number of Caranthian Ore and Crystal deposits. It is up to Zax to find the required amount in the world around him.

Items available in the Materials Generator are divided into the Weapons, Shields, Power Generators, and Miscellaneous:

  
 


 
  The following procedure demonstrates how to create an item(specifically a Modified Laser Welder) in the Materials Generator.

1. Find your way to the Materials Generator onboard Zelon. It is towards the front of the ship.
2. When the Generator appears, click on the Weapons tab. (note the three other tabs as well)
3. Click on the bar featuring the Modified Laser Welder (note that its background becomes highlighted)
4. Click the Generate Button. If the item is not generated, you do not possess enough Ore or Crystals to create it. The quantity of ore or crystals required to create an item are listed to the far right of the item.
