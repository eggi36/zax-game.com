10- 17- 01

There are two new download Add-ons for Ricochet. You can download the Reactive Music, and High Definition Art add-ons here. All of the music in Ricochet is going to be available for download by the end of the week in MP3 format. We have had requests for CD versions of the music. So we will upload the MP3's and you can make your own.

The on-line manual for Ricochet now also has images next to the powerups. Sorry for not doing that to begin with. I hope that makes identifying the powerups easier.

Stay tuned for more news and information on the RPL (Ricochet Players League).

In Zax news, Gamezone.com gave Zax a 9 out of 10 saying "Playing Zax for ten minutes is more exciting than most action games are from start to finish."
Zax is also currently being translated into Japanese and Taiwanese.


10- 11- 01

Ricochet Version 1.01 is now available for download here . The new version fixes some small bugs including the long load time bug that was reported in the Ricochet Technical Forum. Please report any issues you have with Ricochet in the Ricochet Technical Forum. We will be updating at least once more before opening the RPL.

Zax is now available in the United States. The ship date was yesterday so I believe the stores should have them stocked either today or by the end of the week. We are trying to build up a Zax community, if you have any ideas please e-mail Zach@reflexive.net and be sure to keep tabs in the Zax Forum.

Zax and Ricochet both recieved "Pick" status from CNET. Even more impressive is that both were in the "Spotlight" download section. That is a huge honor for us and we send our thanks to the great people over at CNET.


10- 04- 01

Just as promised Ricochet is now available! Click here to get it right now! You should have plenty of time to practice up before the RPA starts up, where you can win actual prizes!


10- 02- 01

Ricochet is in the final testing stages. Everything is on track for a release before the end of the week.

In other news, Zax should be hitting the stores in America very soon. It has already been released in Germany.

Demolition League has been progressing by leaps and bounds. However, there is still much to do. As soon as we can, we will get a demo up as well as a website section. Look for the webite in a few weeks.
