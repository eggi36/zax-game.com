FAQ
** Note: The Zax CD-Key is found on the back of the Jewel Case not the back of the manual as indicated in the manual.


1. What kind of Game is Zax: The Alien Hunter?
Zax: The Alien Hunter is an Action / Adventure game, with heavy emphasis on the action. We have spent a great deal of time and effort making sure that the player always has a goal and plenty of enemies to deal with. The adventure side comes in with an involving storyline that unfolds over one hundred maps.

2. How many missions does Zax: The Alien Hunter have?
There are 22 missions in the game. Many of these missions have several maps, which means that some of the missions can be completed in a non-linear fashion. That is, what you choose to do first in a given scenario, impacts which order you finish the missions.

3. Is there Multi-player in Zax: The Alien Hunter?
Yes, Zax has multiplayer capabilities. Death Match, Capture The Flag, and our new addition to Multi-player called Salvage King, which is our company favorite!

4. When will a Demo for Zax: The Alien Hunter be Available?
Now! You can download it from the demo link below, or from our downloads page.

5. Will Zax: the Alien Hunter ship with a Level Editor?
There are no plans to ship any of the developing tools with Zax.

6. Is there a story in Zax: The Alien Hunter, or is it just kill all the bad guys?
There is a very compelling story that drives the player throughout the game. We have spent a lot of time developing and scripting special events throughout the game to facilitate telling the story to the player. At the same time, we have tried not to overwhelm the player with a billion sub-plots. We feel there is a nice balance between the story elements and the action pace of the game.

7. When will Zax: the Alien Hunter be released?
Zax will be available in September of 2001. Maybe later depending on where you live.

8. What are the minimum specs for Zax: the Alien Hunter?
Pentium II 233 compatible processor with 32 megs of RAM. Of course if you have a faster machine with more RAM, we won't complain.

9. Can Zax: the Alien Hunter be played over the Internet?
Yes, Zax is playable over the internet. We are estimating a maximum of 16 players per game. However, that will depend on the system specs of machine running the server.

10. Is it fun?
It rocks! We think it'll keep you in front of your PC for hours at a time just so you can see what happens around the next corner. (and there are a lot of corners!)
