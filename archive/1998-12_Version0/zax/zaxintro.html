Zax, Coming 1999-2000.

             Zax is a futuristic soldier/explorer of the Galactic Imperium who crash lands on an uncharted world in dangerous territory. Injured from the crash, his ship badly damaged with rampant system failures, Zax must try to repair his ship on a backwater world about to be vaporized by an impending supernova. With limited resources at his command, and danger around every turn, Zax must utilize all his years of military training to simply survive on this complex planet. A planet of turmoil and power...of ancient religion and wondrous magic where nothing is as it seems. Amidst it all, Zax has but one place he can turn to for help, one potential ally, a single downtrodden race that is desperate for help themselves.

            These people, known as the Korbo, live in a primitive agrarian society and worship an angry god. A powerful and terrifying god that walks among them. A god that the people have started to lose their faith in and see as an angry demon...



Zax discovers a new world


Overview

Zax will support single player, multi-player co-operative, and multi-player competitive play, but with some very distinctive gameplay elements. First and foremost Zax is an action game. The play mechanics, the levels, and the interface are all designed and built to support this.  Players must use their heads solving missions while obtaining experience, different and better weapons types, and assorted objects to complete each level. Throughout the game the player is presented in-engine animation sequences, voice-overs and short cinematics to develop the story. Multi-player functions will be done over the Internet or a LAN, and you will be able to play with up to 3 players co-operatively, or 32 players using a competitive mode.

Below are just a couple of the many enemy characters in Zax.
Om's Overseers are massive ancient bots that enslave whole tribes of Korbo for the needs of Om.  Their duties are mostly to find hiding rebel Korbo and bring them to work in camps for the mining of precious minerals.   These were the first robots constructed after the crash many millenia ago and are held in the highest reverence by Om's devout believers.


Crutch Drones control the jungles of  Kor.  Their main objective is to wait in hiding and fend off Korbo Rebels from invading Om's strongholds.  Crutch Drones are large and fast and attack with a powerful energy weapon.  This weapon is like an appendage protruding from their backs.


The Throne of Om.

Zax can also receive help from the people around him...


The Korbo live in small villages, after being exiled from their ancient cities.

The Alien Chieftain of the Korbo can be your ally, or your enemy... it is your choice.  However, as Zax discovers, he  needs all the friends he can get to save his own skin.

While they are a primitive race with tools and ideas of a peaceful agrarian society, the Ancient Magic that they possess can be of great use to Zax at critical stages of his quest.

 These alien inhabitants also guide you through the levels and narrate as the game progresses.


Zax's Starship, The Zelon

The Zelon is Zax’s starship, and more than just another piece of state-of-the-art military machinery. The Zelon was psychically grafted by the Imperium to Zax’s mental matrix as part of his military training and explorative duties. This psychic link decreases pilot response time, as well as enhancing critical decision-making in the field by increasing the amount of instantly accessible information. 

The Zelon was also given a personality to provide companionship to Imperium personnel. The ship has seemed testy and smart-mouthed at times because of this, but has always gotten right down to business when needed.



You will be able to enter Zax's ship and operate many of it's features that will help him on his quest.
